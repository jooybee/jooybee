## Publish to think, not to impress

The best posts start as notes to yourself. You clarify an argument. You notice a contradiction. You put the cleaned-up version online so future-you (and a few strangers) can reuse it.

When the goal becomes “engagement,” the sentences start hedging. You write for the imaginary audience that might share it, not for the reader who might use it.

## Guardrails that work

- **Draft offline** until the structure holds.
- **One claim per post.** Sub-claims can wait.
- **No apology paragraph** at the top. Start where the idea starts.
- **Edit for verbs.** Soft language is where performance hides.

## On frequency

Cadence helps habits. Cadence is not a brand promise. Missing a week is fine. Publishing empty is not.
