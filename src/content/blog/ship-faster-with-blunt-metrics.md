Speed is not about typing faster. It is about **deciding faster** — and decisions rot when the metrics are polite.

Blunt metrics are numbers you cannot hide behind. They move when the product moves. They embarrass you when the product stalls.

## The weekly loop

Every Monday, before stand-up:

1. Write down last week’s three blunt numbers.
2. Write one sentence: “We will change X because Y moved.”
3. Ship the smallest change that could move Y.
4. On Friday, look again. Keep or kill the change.

No slide deck. No “deep dive next sprint.” If you cannot name the number and the change in one breath, you are not ready to ship.

## What makes a metric blunt

- **Tied to behaviour** you can influence this week
- **Visible** without opening five tools
- **Hard to game** without also improving the product
- **Owned** by a person, not a committee

Vanity metrics fail the second test. “Total signups” goes up when you buy ads. Activation does not. Retention does not.

## Failure mode: too many blunt metrics

Three is a ceiling for the weekly loop. More than that and you are back to a dashboard with better branding.
